Dungeon Master

An AI that tells the world — describes environments, plays NPCs and drives the plot. Write what you do, and the DM answers with consequences. The DM invents the world together with you, not in advance.

🛡️

Lorekeeper

A quiet system that keeps track of the numbers — HP, gold, items, XP. When the DM says you take damage or find treasure, the Lorekeeper updates your character sheet automatically. You never need to count yourself.

🎲

Dice & Mechanics

When the outcome is uncertain, the DM calls for a dice roll. A roll button appears in the chat — click it, or choose ✍️ Write instead to act freely. Nat 20 = triumph, nat 1 = catastrophe. Type /roll 1d20+4 to roll whenever you want.
