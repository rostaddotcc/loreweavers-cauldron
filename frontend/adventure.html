v1.3 — The Rewoven Thread
  • Undo your last turn — a mistyped line or a decision made too fast can be rewound: the message, the DM's reply and everything the Lorekeeper recorded from it. Inventory, gold, HP, quests and memory return to where they stood. It costs one turn, and only the most recent turn can be rewound.
  • Qwen 3.8 Flash joins the free table — a fast mind with real reasoning, free for every account. Pick it in Settings or when you start a new adventure.
  • The party portrait blends again — the character card had gone opaque; the portrait now sits behind the name, class and health bars the way it used to.
  • Your blade rolls, the DM does not — weapon damage now tumbles through the server's dice engine on every hit, doubled on a critical. The story decides what it feels like; the dice decide what it costs.
  • Conditions that actually bind — prone, restrained, stunned and poisoned change rolls, turns and HP between rounds. A knocked-down goblin is easier to hit, and it hits worse.
  • Level-ups that level up — new tiers grant hit-dice health, class features, spell slots and a proficiency bonus that finally scales. Armour moves your AC; long rests hand back half your hit dice.
  • A rulebook that matches the machine — the mechanics page was audited line by line against the code; spell slots and rests are documented at last, and offline rolls say so honestly.
  • One gold across the realm — shared palette tokens, HP bars that update every combat turn, quests that pay what they promise, and a purse that can no longer be quietly rounded to zero.

Dungeon Master

An AI that tells the world — describes environments, plays NPCs and drives the plot. Just write what you do in plain words, the way you'd speak at a real table. No syntax, no commands — the DM understands intent and answers with consequences.

🦉

The Lorekeeper

A quiet scribe that keeps the books — HP, gold, items, XP, quests, locations. When the DM says you take damage or find treasure, the Lorekeeper updates everything in the background. You never track numbers yourself; the world remembers.

🎲

Dice & Mechanics

When the outcome is uncertain, the DM calls for a roll and a Roll or Write card appears in the chat. Click the dice to roll, or the pen to describe your attempt in words instead. Nat 20 = triumph, nat 1 = catastrophe.
