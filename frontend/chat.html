🧠 About the Models
The Dungeon Master writes the world. The Lorekeeper quietly tracks HP, gold, XP and items in the background. The extraction model handles facts, the journal and summaries. You can switch the DM and Lorekeeper in Settings ⚙️ (the DM choice also appears when you start a new adventure).
qwen3.8-max
The flagship. Deep reasoning, rich narration, fewest mistakes — but it thinks long and hard before every reply.⏱ slowest · best quality · thinks a lot
qwen3.6-flash
The balanced storyteller. Visible reasoning, solid quality, noticeably faster.⏱ medium · good quality · thinks
deepseek-v4-flash
The quick hand. Fast, economical replies with light reasoning — great for casual scenes.⏱ fast · economical · light thinking
step-3.7-flash
Another fast option. Snappy narration; occasionally needs a retry on complex character generation.⏱ fast · light thinking
ollama:heretic
Your local oracle, running on this server. Free and private — but slow, and answers on CPU time.⏱ local · free · slowest

Why does a "high reasoning" model take longer? Before answering, the model works through the scene — the campaign state, your choices, the rules — and you can watch it think in the reasoning bubble. More thinking means a richer, more consistent reply, but also a longer wait (sometimes 30–90 seconds). Fast models answer in seconds but with less depth. If a reply ever feels slow, that's the DM working — or switch to a faster model in Settings ⚙️.
