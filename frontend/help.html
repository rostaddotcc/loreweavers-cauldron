⚔ How to Play

A guide to the adventure — from first login to the final dice roll.

Core Loop

1.The DM awakens — asks 2-3 questions about your character and the world 2.You answer — the DM weaves your answers into the opening scene 3.You act — write freely: fight, sneak, speak, explore 4.The DM responds — describes consequences, NPCs, new choices 5.Dice — when the outcome is uncertain, the DM calls for a roll and you roll 6.Repeat — the story is built from your choices, nothing is predetermined

How Turns Work

Every exchange at the table counts as one turn: your action, followed by the DM's response.

  • The current turn is shown in the chat header: Campaign: … · Turn N
  • Turns are counted automatically — you never have to track them yourself
  • Checkpoints (/save) remember which turn you saved at, so you can always see where the story stands

Getting Started

  • Log in with your username and password
  • Choose your adventure type: Prepare (build a world), Import (upload files), or New (freestyle)
  • Create your character: choose an archetype or write freely, pick a model (DeepSeek V4 Flash / Qwen 3.8 Max)
  • Click "Summon Character" — the AI generates a complete character sheet
  • Click "To the Table" — the DM awakens and asks its questions

Talking to NPCs

Type @Name in the chat to address an NPC directly.

Example: @Lyra Vane "What are you doing here?"

The NPC answers in its own voice. The DM can step in with narration at any time.

You get autocomplete when you type @ — every known NPC is listed.

Dice

  • When the outcome is uncertain, the DM calls for a roll — e.g. [ROLL: 1d20+4 | DEXTERITY]
  • A roll button appears in the chat — click it to roll
  • The dice tray at the bottom is locked (🔒) until the DM calls for a roll
  • You can also type /roll 1d20+4 — the roll command (alias /rulla) — directly in the chat to roll whenever you want
  • Natural 20 = CRITICAL! Natural 1 = PATHETIC!

The Guardian

The Guardian is the keeper of mechanics. While the DM narrates, the Guardian works in the background — applying damage, healing, XP, gold, items and quests to your character.

  • Mechanical effects appear as a 🛡️ Guardian Report card in the chat (e.g. the combat log)
  • A pulsing indicator shows the Guardian is processing — keep playing, the story never stops for bookkeeping
  • Your character sheet updates automatically: HP, inventory, quests, NPCs, locations

Mechanical Tags (Invisible)

The DM uses tags that the system strips from the text and converts into effects:

  • [DAMAGE:12] — you take 12 damage (HP decreases)
  • [HEAL:8] — you are healed 8 HP
  • [XP:250] — you gain experience points
  • [GOLD:50] — you find gold
  • [ITEM:Sword|Weapon|rare] — new item in your inventory
  • [QUEST:Save the village|Description|100 gp] — new quest
  • [NPC:Name|Role|relation] — a new NPC is registered
  • [LOCATION:Stone Circle] — the location updates

Models

You can switch the AI model mid-adventure via the dropdown in the top bar.

  • DeepSeek V4 Flash — standard, fast, reasoning model (thinks before answering)
  • Qwen 3.8 Max — powerful, great at creative narration
  • Qwen 3.6 Flash — fast, used for atmosphere/ASCII art
  • Local models — via Ollama (requires a local server)

Your choice is saved in the browser and stays until you change it.

Music & Sound

  • ♫ — music on/off (mood-based loops: night, day, battle, town, rest, dungeon)
  • 🔊 — sound effects on/off (dice, coins, battle, level-up)
  • The music changes mood automatically based on the DM's text

Rules (D&D 5e, Simplified)

  • Roll: 1d20 + modifier vs DC (Difficulty Class)
  • DC 10 = easy, DC 15 = medium, DC 20 = hard
  • HP 0 = death saves (1d20, 3 successes = stable, 3 failures = dead)
  • XP = experience points, level-up at thresholds (300, 900, 2700...)
  • Combat: initiative → turn order → attack → damage → repeat
  • Rest: short rest (1h) = restore HP, long rest (8h) = full HP + spell slots

The DM is the final judge. The rules are a framework, not a prison.
