⚔ How to Play

A guide to the adventure — from first login to the final dice roll.

Core Loop

1.The DM awakens — asks 2-3 questions about your character and the world 2.You answer — the DM weaves your answers into the opening scene 3.You act — write freely: fight, sneak, speak, explore 4.The DM responds — describes consequences, NPCs, new choices 5.Dice — when the outcome is uncertain, the DM calls for a roll and you roll 6.Repeat — the story is built from your choices, nothing is predetermined

How Turns Work

Every exchange at the table counts as one turn: your action, followed by the DM's response.

  • The current turn is shown in the chat header: Campaign: … · Turn N
  • Turns are counted automatically — you never have to track them yourself
  • Checkpoints (/save) remember which turn you saved at, so you can always see where the story stands

Getting Started

  • Log in with your username and password
  • Choose your adventure type: Prepare (build a world with prompt + files) or New (freestyle)
  • Create your character: choose an archetype or write freely, pick a model, click "Summon Character"
  • The AI generates a complete character sheet
  • Click "To the Table" — the DM awakens and asks its questions

Talking to NPCs

Type @Name in the chat to address an NPC directly.

Example: @Lyra Vane "What are you doing here?"

The NPC answers in its own voice. The DM can step in with narration at any time.

You get autocomplete when you type @ — every known NPC is listed.

Dice

  • When the outcome is uncertain, the DM calls for a roll — a roll button appears in the chat
  • Click it to roll — or choose ✍️ Write instead to act without rolling (the chat unlocks and you narrate freely)
  • The chat is locked while a roll is pending, so the dice stay fair and deliberate
  • You can also type /roll 1d20+4 — the roll command (alias /rulla) — directly in the chat to roll whenever you want
  • Natural 20 = CRITICAL! Natural 1 = PATHETIC!

The Lorekeeper

The Lorekeeper is the keeper of mechanics. While the DM narrates, the Lorekeeper works in the background — applying damage, healing, XP, gold, items and quests to your character.

  • Mechanical effects appear as a 🦉 Lorekeeper Report card in the chat (e.g. the combat log)
  • A pulsing indicator shows the Lorekeeper is processing — keep playing, the story never stops for bookkeeping
  • Your character sheet updates automatically: HP, inventory, quests, NPCs, locations

Mechanical Tags (Invisible)

The DM's narration is laced with hidden markers that the system converts into real effects — you never see them:

  • Damage & healing — your HP changes automatically
  • XP & gold — gained and tracked for you
  • Items — new equipment appears in your inventory
  • Quests — new goals appear in your journal
  • NPCs — characters you meet are registered
  • Locations — the map updates as you travel

Models

You can switch the AI model mid-adventure from the Settings (⚙️) menu in the top bar — pick a new Dungeon Master or Lorekeeper model at any time.

  • qwen3.8-max — powerful, great at creative narration
  • qwen3.6-flash — fast, reliable all-rounder
  • deepseek-v4-flash-0731 — fast reasoning model (thinks before answering)
  • step-3.7-flash — fast, budget option

Your choice is saved and stays until you change it. If the DM stops responding, try switching models here first.

Music & Sound

  • ♫ — music on/off (mood-based loops: night, day, battle, town, rest, dungeon)
  • 🔊 — sound effects on/off (dice, coins, battle, level-up)
  • The music changes mood automatically based on the DM's text

Rules (D&D 5e, Simplified)

  • Roll: 1d20 + modifier vs DC (Difficulty Class)
  • DC 10 = easy, DC 15 = medium, DC 20 = hard
  • HP 0 = death saves (1d20, 3 successes = stable, 3 failures = dead)
  • XP = experience points, level-up at thresholds (300, 900, 2700...)
  • Combat: initiative → turn order → attack → damage → repeat
  • Rest: short rest (1h) = restore HP, long rest (8h) = full HP + spell slots

The DM is the final judge. The rules are a framework, not a prison.

Tips

  • Write freely — you can attempt anything (you may try, you may not always succeed)
  • Ask the Rules Oracle (📜) if you are unsure about D&D rules
  • Explore the Map (🗺️) to see locations and travel times
  • Read the Logbook (📜) for a summary of the adventure
  • Save your character in the Vault (🏰) to reuse it
  • Export (📦) to download the entire campaign as a ZIP

If Something Goes Wrong

  • No reply from the DM? The chat shows the underlying error — an HTTP code like HTTP 429 (rate limit), HTTP 500 (server), HTTP 402 (billing), or a network/empty-response message
  • If the issue persists, try changing models in the Settings (⚙️) — a different model often routes around the problem
  • Connection dropped mid-reply? The chat recovers the DM's answer from the transcript automatically
  • Check the Engine Room (🛠️) in Settings for live debug logs if things look wrong
