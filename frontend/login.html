What Brews Inside

Not a chatbot. A campaign.

Most AI games are a conversation you close and forget. The Cauldron is a text-based table — you write, the DM answers in kind, and a hidden engine keeps the whole world turning between your visits.

❦

Purely Text-Based

No combat maps, no miniatures, no busywork — the entire game is played in words. You write what you do, the DM narrates what happens, and your imagination renders the graphics. The oldest kind of role-playing, powered by the newest kind of mind.

A Persistent World

Behind the scenes, a complex AI system keeps the books — story threads, characters, growth and relationships are tracked across every session. Leave for a season; the innkeeper still remembers what you did, and who you did it with.

Create Your Own Avatars

Give every face at the table a portrait. Your hero, the NPCs you meet, even the DM — each avatar is painted by AI from the character’s own story, and can be rerolled until it feels right. StepFun painting unlocks with the one-time Support top-up (3€) — and Patron (one-time 10€) unlocks Wan 2.7 Pro, the premium image engine.

Strict D&D 5e Ruleset

Initiative, action economy, advantage, saving throws — real rolls with real stakes. The DM narrates everything; the Lorekeeper keeps the books. A natural 20 still feels like lightning.

The Forge — Carry Your Legend

Every hero you summon is saved in The Forge — full sheet, level, items, gold and story intact. Start a new adventure any time and walk back in as the same adventurer: your history travels with you, the new world has no idea what you're capable of.
