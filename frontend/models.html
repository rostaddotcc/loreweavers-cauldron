The Minds Behind the Mask

✦

Seven minds can sit at your table — and behind them wait the voices and the brushes. Each DM is a different weight of thought, a different pace of speech; the narrators give the realm a voice, and the painters give it faces. Here is what each one is made of, and what it is best at.
Powered by Qwen, DeepSeek, StepFun & Wan

⚙

How the Table Works

🧙The Dungeon Master — the voice you hear. Every turn, your words go to the DM model, which narrates the world, plays the NPCs and drives the story. This is the model you choose at the model gate when you start a campaign — and you can change it at any time in the settings.
🦉The Lorekeeper (Guardian) — the quiet bookkeeper. After every exchange, a separate model reads the scene and updates the world state: HP, gold, items, XP, quests, locations. It works in the background, so you never have to track numbers yourself — the world simply remembers.
✎The Extraction model — the scribe that turns free-form actions into mechanics. When you write "I draw my sword and lunge", extraction decides what that means in dice terms, damage and consequence — keeping the 5e rules honest even when you don't use the exact phrasing.
⏱Response time — heavier minds think longer. A turn with the fast StepFun 3.7 Flash usually answers in a few seconds; Qwen 3.8 Max may pause several seconds more because it reasons deeply before it speaks. The Lorekeeper and Extraction run in parallel after the DM replies, so they rarely add visible wait — but a long, action-heavy turn can take a little longer overall. Pick the fastest mind that still feels like your storyteller.
