StepFun 3.7 Flash

SF
StepFun 3.7 Flash
StepFun · released May 29, 2026
Steadfast DM

The steadfast Dungeon Master — the default voice at every free table. Fast, dependable, and built for structured play: it runs the rules engine, the Guardian, and the extraction of every scene into the world state. A sparse mixture-of-experts model that wakes only a sliver of itself per token, it answers with a brisk, reliable cadence and offers three selectable reasoning levels (low, medium, high) so you can trade depth for speed.

The swift mind — a budget-conscious alternative from the Qwen family. It reads text, images and video, holds a full 1M-token context window, and pairs a hybrid linear-attention core with a MoE body so it stays fast on long campaigns. The economical choice when you want Alibaba’s storytelling without the heavyweight bill.

Qwen 3.8 Max

Q
Qwen 3.8 Max
Alibaba Qwen · flagship, generally available Aug 3, 2026
The Grand DM

The grand mind — Alibaba’s 2.4-trillion-parameter flagship, a sparse MoE that activates only 95 billion parameters per request. A full 1M-token context window (roughly 750,000 words — entire books at once), deep reasoning that always thinks, and the richest prose of the four. When your campaign deserves the full ceremony, this is the voice that delivers it.
