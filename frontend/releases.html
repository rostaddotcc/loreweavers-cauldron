The Honest Dice

COMBAT The engine rolls every blade
  • New Your weapon damage is now rolled, not narrated — a hit tumbles your wielded weapon's damage dice through the server's own dice engine; a critical hit doubles them. No story number decides your damage any more.
  • Fixed The war council wakes up — enemy attacks rolled by code now update HP bars, initiative and the combat log every turn; they used to go silent between rounds.
  • Fixed One law per combatant — the Dungeon Master now narrates intent and impression only; the engine reports the outcome, and the story weaves it in next turn. Prose and numbers can no longer disagree.
RULES Conditions live, rests true, levels real
  • New Prone, restrained, stunned, poisoned — they bite now — a knocked-down foe is easier to hit and its own blows falter; stunned combatants lose their turn; venom burns between rounds.
  • New Level-ups grant everything you are owed — hit-dice health, class features, spell slots and a rising proficiency bonus, whichever road the experience arrives by. A fifth-level character finally rolls +3.
  • Fixed Armour changes your armour class — swapping into the chain you picked up moves your AC; it used to freeze at whatever the forge wrote on day one.
  • Fixed Long rests give back half your hit dice, as the table intends, and every rest refills spell slots and dulls exhaustion.
  • Fixed Quests pay what they promise — completion grants its stored XP and gold on every path, once, never twice.
  • Fixed Your purse can no longer be quietly destroyed — spending more than you carry is refused, not rounded to zero; the coins stay.
TRUTH A rulebook that matches the machine
  • Improved The mechanics page was audited line by line against the code and rewritten where it lied — spell slots, rests and the dice contract are documented at last, and nothing on the page promises an engine that does not run.
  • Fixed Rolls you take while offline are marked honest — if the server cannot be reached, the dice fall locally and the chat says so.
  • Fixed Dying reads as dying — at 0 HP the chronicle shows the death-save tracker, and the Dungeon Master knows you are down instead of asking you to stroll.
  • Fixed Signup promises retired cleanly — the old welcome-turns text is gone from every page and search snippet; the free road is 50 fresh turns daily, one turn per message.
  • Improved The realm speaks one tongue per toggle — quota warnings, the day-cap modal and idle whispers are now translated both ways, and the legacy welcome-bonus line in your profile says exactly what it is.
CRAFT One gold, one ledger, less noise
  • Improved A single palette across the realm — three competing golds became one; every page now draws from the same tokens so the torchlight finally matches.
  • Improved Carried weight counts your coins — the sheet, the Dungeon Master and the Guardian now agree on how heavy your pack is.
  • Fixed Returning heroes skip the tutorial — the onboarding steps fold away once you have an adventure; one line invites a new one.
  • Fixed A silent-turn plague cured — malformed numbers from the story-tellers no longer drop a whole turn's XP, gold and items on the floor; they are repaired or refused.

MECHANICS The whole 5e table, in play

The rules are no longer a suggestion the Dungeon Master reads from memory — they are a contract the realm enforces. Every major mechanic a table needs now lives in the game, verified by tests, and applied by the Lorekeeper after every turn.

  • FEATSkills and proficiency — the right tool for the right task: skill checks carry proficiency and ability modifiers, and the DM knows when the party is pushing its luck.
  • FEATSpell slots spend — casting a spell consumes the slot, and the spellbook tracks what is left. No more infinite fireballs.
  • FEATInspiration and class features — the spark of a nat-20 is banked as inspiration, and class features arrive as real rules instead of flavor text.
  • FEATConditions that tick — blinded, grappled, restrained and the rest apply their effects and expire on schedule.
  • FEATResistance and vulnerability — fireball into a creature that shrugs fire halves the pain; a vampire still fears the sun's light twice as much.
  • FEATEncumbrance, exhaustion, cover, darkvision — carry too much and movement suffers; push too hard and exhaustion stacks; half cover means half the hits land; darkvision decides what you see in the dark.
  • FEATGrapple, shove, movement, downtime training — the physical tools of the table: lock a foe in a grapple, shove them off a ledge, move with the terrain, and spend downtime learning a tool or language.
  • AIBattle AI for enemies — when enemies are alive, the Guardian decides their moves and the code rolls the dice — no enemy ever hits or misses by LLM fiat.
DICE Dice the realm can trust
  • FIXServer-authoritative dice — every player roll now travels to the realm's own dice engine (secrets, not a client-side random) before the result lands in the chat. Advantage rolls two d20s server-side, then picks the best.
  • FEATThe DM dice ceremony — when the Dungeon Master rolls for an enemy or an ally, the dice tumble visibly in the chat: a flicker, a roll, and the result — no more placeholder boxes.
  • FIXNo double damage — a player or ally attack that already dealt its damage can no longer strike the same enemy twice through the mechanics layer.
  • FIXDead enemies stay dead — a slain creature's last rolls no longer re-render on every new reply.
MEMORY A memory that holds

The realm's memory was rebuilt from the inside: a moving window of facts, ranked fresh before every single turn, backed by a semantic index that is actually reachable from the cauldron.

  • FEATFact register — the moving window — every turn's events are distilled into durable facts (people, places, promises, grudges). Before each reply, the top 8 facts are scored against your message — keyword overlap, recency, mentions, confidence — and only the winners enter the prompt.
  • AISemantic recall, fixed — the Qdrant index (transcript, lore, summaries) was unreachable from inside the container; the connection now works, and the top 4 semantic chunks join the fact window every turn. RAG is no longer a checkbox — it fires on every DM call.
  • FEATNothing is deleted, everything competes — facts fall out of the window but never out of the register: compaction every 50 turns marks stale facts, superseded facts are retired, and player-pinned facts (the /pin command) can never be evicted.
  • FIXTransparent recall — the Engine Room now shows exactly what the DM saw: 8 facts + 4 RAG chunks per call, so you can watch the realm remember.
  • FIXCampaign language held — scene, chapter and arc summaries now follow the campaign's language instead of hardcoded Swedish; NPC keys survive apostrophes and parentheses.
TURNS A turn ledger that spends honestly
  • FEATOne turn per message, nothing hidden — your chat message costs exactly 1 turn. The guardian, threads, summaries, day-entries and battle AI run free inside it — the internal machinery is never billed against you.
  • FEATTTS counts once, cache hits free — a synthesized voice costs 1 turn the first time; replaying a cached clip costs nothing.
  • FEATImage turns — painted portraits charge a single turn, with the price shown before you paint.
  • UXQuota you can read — hover the turn pill for the honest breakdown, see the big refresh timer, and open the full profile modal from the top bar: turns, tier, and subscription in one place.
  • UXFree models, everywhere — OpenRouter free models now sit in the regular DM, Guardian and Extraction pickers (and character creation) — no separate experiment row — with backend validation for every tier.
MOBILE The table fits in your hand

  • UXTTS controls as chips — the voice boxes became chip-sized (26px), the media player a minimal pill, and the combat button is icon-only in the bar — the whole stack is radically shorter.
  • FIXBottom nav that stays put — the nav is now flow-based instead of fixed-position, so Firefox Android no longer hides it behind the URL bar.

The realm now carries its own keep. After a season of free tables, the forge accepts coin — and with it, the fire burns steadier. Every path is real, wired end-to-end through Stripe, and every adventurer keeps a free road forever.
