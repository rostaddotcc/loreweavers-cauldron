0.7

The Forge, Earning

PAY The Cauldron takes a payment

The realm now carries its own keep. After a season of free tables, the forge accepts coin — and with it, the fire burns steadier. Every path is real, wired end-to-end through Stripe, and every adventurer keeps a free road forever.

  • FEATFounding offer — two free months — for the first seven days, subscribe for one month and get 2 months free on Companion. And Lifetime for 50€ — half price, one-time. The countdown lives on the login page and the table.
  • FEATFour paths into the realm — Wanderer (free), Companion, Adventurer, and Lifetime. Each a real tier: free stays free, the paid paths unlock the deeper models and the twin voices.
  • FEATStripe checkout, live — the pricing page is wired to a real Stripe account: products, prices, and a webhook that records payment. No more mock buttons.
  • FEATWebhook idempotency — a delivered payment event is recorded exactly once, even if Stripe retries it. The ledger never double-counts a coin.
  • FEATLifetime is truly forever — a Lifetime purchase never expires (holds until the end of time, not a date). Granting a tier writes a line into the player's transcript.
  • AIModel gating by tier — free players travel with the steadfast step-3.7-flash; paid paths unlock DeepSeek, Qwen 3.8 Max, and Qwen 3.6 Flash as their Dungeon Master. Locked models are visibly grayed out, never silently ignored.
  • UXYour tier is visible — the profile card in the gear menu shows your path, and the pricing page marks your current subscription with a lit sigil.

The Free Road

FREE A fair ration, honestly counted
  • FEATTurn counter in the header — turn, day, free turns left, and the reset date, all visible at a glance. On mobile it tucks into the menu so the top bar stays clean.
  • FEATDaily renewal — your 50 turns refresh each day at midnight, whether you were here or not. A cron keeps the dates in step, and the count rolls over lazily even if the cron sleeps.
  • UXAn honest cap modal — when the free turns run out, the game tells you plainly: new turns on the reset date, or upgrade for unlimited. No dead buttons, no tricks.
  • PAYAdmin top-ups — the realm-keeper can grant bonus turns (consumed before the cap) or reset a player's count, without touching the monthly ceiling.
