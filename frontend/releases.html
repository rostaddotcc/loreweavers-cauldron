RELEASE NOTES

What's new in the realm — from v0.5 to v0.6.

v0.6.0

The Production Readiness Update — themes, releases, transparency, and a heap of polish.

  • Theme switcher — five dark-fantasy palettes (Terminal / Blood / Arcane / Ember / Iron), one click away in the top bar. Your choice is remembered.
  • This release page — every update now comes with notes.
  • Model transparency — a guide to the Dungeon Master models, and why "high reasoning" means a slightly longer wait for a richer reply.
  • Longer soundscapes — new 10+ minute ambient loops (classical MIDI, public domain, rendered in 16-bit spirit).
  • Dead code purge — ~1,600 lines of orphaned CSS/JS removed; three corrupted stylesheet comments repaired; a hidden Escape-key crash fixed.

What changed since v0.5

Combat — v26 "Chat-First"

  • FEAT The DM narrates everything; the Guardian extracts the mechanics — no hidden battle AI deciding your fate.
  • FEAT Turn engine: initiative order, action economy, and proper turn rotation.
  • FEAT Inline combat UI: round banners, enemy cards, HP-diff lines, and a status bar above the input (R3 │ enemy 12/24 │ Du 18/22).
  • FIX Initiative reveal, awakening re-trigger, HP null crashes, log-size limits, and player-HP sync in the combat tag.

Design & UX — the Codex era

  • UX The Codex: one tabbed modal for Character, NPCs, Map, Journal and Facts — no more page-hopping.
  • UX Slim sidebar with party, quests, known NPCs and location; mobile drawer + bottom nav + sheets.
  • UX Unified warm dark-fantasy palette on every page; pixel sprites replace emojis; site-wide font switcher (7 typefaces).
  • UX Readable type scale (no more 0.93rem body text) and a flat, minimal Engine Room console.
  • FEAT Uploadable chat avatars (44px, framed, role-colored).

The Dungeon Master

  • AI DM prompts v23→v26: DC ladder, the DM Triad / Rule of Cool, 5e resting, advantage/disadvantage, quick rules, world-consistency name rules.
  • AI Visible reasoning bubble — watch the DM think, with token/time readout in the reply footer.
  • AI Dynamic narration length: short for action, long-form (4k tokens) for lore, books, legends.
  • AI Model gate at adventure start: pick your DM model (admin chooses the Guardian freely).
  • AI Guardian runs in the background, never blocking the reply — with a polling indicator and in-chat Guardian timeline.
  • AI Character generation: forbidden-name lists + higher temperature for varied, unexpected names.

Rolls, items & the weight of things

  • FEAT The D20 ceremony — an inline 3-second dice ritual with flicker, glitter, and nat-20/nat-1 spectacle (D20, not D6).
  • FEAT DM-requested rolls appear as inline buttons; /rulla 1d20+4 works anytime; advantage rolls supported.
  • FEAT Clickable item cards with D&D stats; item detail modal; roll buttons on usable items.
  • FEAT Carry weight (STR × 15 lbs), coin weight, item lore — every item has a history.
  • FIX The DM now sees your full inventory (not just equipped) and always keeps starting gear.

Sound & Voice

  • SOUND Typewriter reveal with 16-bit "midi" ticks; NPC quote bubbles with per-NPC colors; pixel-particle bursts on every message.
  • SOUND Synthesized SFX (Web Audio) — coins, crits, battle jingles — zero audio files.
  • SOUND Mood music loops that follow the scene (night/day/battle/dungeon/town/rest).
  • SOUND TTS via Qwen-Audio (Token Plan) — play button on DM replies, male/female voices, autoplay toggle, cache.

Language & accounts

  • FEAT English-first UI with Swedish fallback; language is per-campaign and survives new games.
  • FEAT Self-registration; admin dashboard (players, campaigns, token usage per model); per-user turn caps.
  • FIX Campaign state isolation, race-condition locks, and English debug logs throughout the backend.

Notable fixes

  • Raw HTTP errors surface in chat (402 / 429 / 500 / network) with a "try changing models in Settings" hint.
  • Roll buttons and Guardian reports no longer fire mid-typewriter; the chat always pins to the bottom.
  • Mobile: no iOS auto-zoom, safe-area padding, 44px tap targets, drawers/sheets z-order fixed.
  • Logbook/facts ordering, map clipping, flexbox starvation — the usual suspects, banished.
